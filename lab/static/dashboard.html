Blue: items sorted in the last minute. Grey: overall effectiveness (OEE, right axis, 0-1). The shift's KPIs are written every simulated second by factory/ops/shift.py to runs/<shift>/kpis.jsonl; this page polls /api/shift.

What the numbers mean

Availability: share of the shift the robot was not stopped or recovering. Performance: items the robot handled (picked at least once) out of all that arrived; the rest passed while it was busy (capacity). Quality: of the items it handled, the share that ended in the right chute. OEE = availability × performance × quality, the factory's standard one-number summary. The operations report puts these against belt speed, arrival rate and the cell's versions.
